About Our Project

Teachers use computers, smartphones and online platforms for their daily academic work.

But using the internet also comes with risks. This project studies cyber awareness among teachers and their knowledge of online safety.

What This Project Explores

Digital Awareness

Understanding how academicians use digital platforms in their academic activities.

Cyber Threats

Identifying common cyber incidents, online frauds and security concerns.

Data Protection

Understanding the importance of protecting student records and institutional information.

Cyber Training

Exploring cybersecurity training requirements among teachers.
