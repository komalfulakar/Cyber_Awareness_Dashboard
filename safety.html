Cyber Safety & Security Guidelines

Simple and practical cybersecurity habits can help academicians protect their personal information, student records and institutional data.

Essential Cyber Safety Practices

01. Strong Passwords

Create strong and unique passwords for different accounts. Use multi-factor authentication wherever available.

02. Identify Phishing

Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments. Verify the sender before sharing information.

03. Protect Student Data

Keep student records confidential. Share academic information only with authorised individuals.

04. Secure Devices

Keep computers and smartphones updated. Use screen locks and trusted security software.

05. Safe Internet Usage

Avoid accessing sensitive accounts through unsecured public Wi-Fi. Use trusted networks.

06. Responsible AI Usage

Avoid entering confidential student or institutional information into public AI tools.

07. Regular Backups

Maintain secure backups of important academic documents and institutional files.

08. Report Cybercrime

Report suspicious activities and cyber incidents promptly through appropriate institutional or official reporting channels.

Remember: Stay Alert, Stay Secure!

Cybersecurity is a shared responsibility. Small safety habits can make a significant difference in creating a safer digital learning environment.
