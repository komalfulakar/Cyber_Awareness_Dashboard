About Our Project

Understanding cybersecurity awareness and digital safety requirements among academicians.

Project Overview

Cybersecurity is becoming increasingly important in the modern education system. Educational institutions depend on digital platforms, online learning tools, cloud services and AI-based applications.

These technologies create new opportunities but also introduce cybersecurity risks involving personal information, student records, research data and institutional systems.

Objectives of the Survey

01. Understand Digital Usage

Identify how frequently academicians use digital platforms and technologies.

02. Identify Cyber Threats

Understand cyber incidents and security concerns experienced by academicians.

03. Evaluate Awareness

Understand confidence in identifying cyber threats and protecting sensitive data.

04. Identify Training Needs

Explore preferred cybersecurity training topics and institutional support requirements.

Survey Methodology

The project uses a structured questionnaire distributed through Google Forms to collect information from academicians.

The responses will be organized, analyzed and presented through an interactive website dashboard.

Target participants: Approximately 100 academicians.

Project Collaboration

This cybersecurity awareness activity is conducted in collaboration with Softsense Technoserve (India) Pvt. Ltd.
