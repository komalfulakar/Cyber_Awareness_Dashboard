Cyber Awareness Among Academicians

A study about cyber safety and online awareness among teachers and professors.

About Our Project

Teachers use computers, smartphones and the internet for teaching and other academic activities.

But the internet also has some risks. This project focuses on cyber awareness among teachers and how they stay safe online.

What We Study

Digital Usage

How teachers use computers, smartphones and online platforms.

Online Threats

Common problems like phishing, online fraud and fake messages.

Data Safety

Protecting personal information and college records.

Cyber Awareness

Knowledge of online safety and the need for cyber training.
